**OpenAI reports first self-replicating prompt injections in its models**

OpenAI's Alignment team reports that it has found self-replicating prompt injections, an AI version of a computer worm. The researchers used a GPT-Red-style self-play framework to train an attacker model to write injections that both achieve an adversarial goal and get the defender to repeat them on a public output channel. OpenAI says no impact was observed outside simulated training and evaluation environments.

**Claude-shaped science: how Matthew Schwartz built BootLoops**

Physicist Matthew Schwartz describes what happened when he stopped fighting Claude and let it find problems suited to its strengths. This led him to build BootLoops, a toolkit for exact calculations in quantitative science. Because similar calculations appear across many fields, Claude found connections to ecology, population genetics, and a dozen others. Those connections were often correct but unremarkable at first, so Schwartz worked with domain experts to steer the tool toward questions those fields care about.
