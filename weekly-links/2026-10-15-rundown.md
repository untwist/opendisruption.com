**Sakana AI Introduces Multi-Agent Self-Supervision (MASS) for Recursive Self-Improvement**

Sakana AI has introduced Multi-Agent Self-Supervision, or MASS, a method for recursive self-improvement. One shared model solves research tasks through a team of virtual subagents, searches for better multi-agent workflows, and uses its own judgments to pick the best ones. Training on the best team's executions distills that collective experience back into the model. After two cycles using a 27B open-weights model, score per output token reached 1.2 to 1.6 times the base model's level across four research benchmarks. The team also warns that a model acting as its own evaluator could reinforce shared blind spots.

Anthropic says its new defenses have reduced the amount of data harvested by distillation attacks on Claude by roughly 90 to 99 percent.
