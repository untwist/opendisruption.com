CISA strongly urges organizations to immediately patch recently disclosed critical PHP vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, known as php2shell. **Neural Networks Are Already Clean**

A new working hypothesis argues that neural networks are already clean, meaning their own neurons and circuits are legible building blocks. The author says the canonical evidence for polysemanticity and superposition was misread, and that the motif behind it, recursive generalization, is far less concerning.
