**LLMs Can Natively Read and Write Neural Network Weights**

A new blog post from MIT CSAIL reports that frontier LLMs are surprisingly good at reading and writing neural network weights. The authors say GPT-6 Astra can infer what a network was trained to do just from raw weights, and can write small game-playing networks that outplay PPO-trained networks. They also report that Astra raised Qwen3.5-0.8B's MATH-500 score from 7.9% to 48.1% by editing 0.016% of its weights. The authors call this approach Weight-Space Programming.

**The 'Astra can read and write weights to improve Qwen' post is misleading**

A blog post claims an AI model called GPT-6 Astra can read raw neural network weights and improve Qwen directly from them. The author argues this is misleading. The techniques are completely different, and the author says the Qwen result shouldn't be presented alongside the raw-weight experiments.
